# Food Delivery Data Visualization Portfolio – Day 14

## Overview

This project performs data visualization and exploratory analysis on a Food Delivery Business Performance Dataset using Python, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

The objective is to visualize important business patterns and understand how factors such as orders, revenue, marketing spend, delivery time, customer ratings, weather, cities, cuisines, and order channels affect performance.

## Dataset

- **Rows:** 360
- **Columns:** 16
- **Format:** CSV

### Important Variables

- Order Batch ID
- Date
- City
- Cuisine
- Order Channel
- Weather
- Orders
- Average Order Value
- Revenue
- Marketing Spend
- Discounts
- Average Delivery Minutes
- Customer Rating
- Repeat Customer Percentage

## Visualizations Created

The notebook includes:

1. Line plot – Orders over time
2. Line plot – Revenue over time
3. Bar chart – Revenue by city
4. Bar chart – Orders by cuisine
5. Scatter plot – Marketing spend vs revenue
6. Scatter plot – Delivery time vs customer rating
7. Histogram – Order distribution
8. Box plot – Revenue distribution by city
9. Violin plot – Average order value by cuisine
10. Count plot – Order channels by weather
11. Bar chart – Revenue by weather
12. Bar chart – Revenue by order channel
13. Correlation heatmap
14. City and cuisine revenue heatmap
15. Discount vs no-discount comparison
16. Final data-driven findings

## Key Findings

The analysis identifies important patterns in:

- Revenue and order trends over time
- Differences in performance between cities
- Popular cuisines based on order volume
- Relationship between marketing expenditure and revenue
- Relationship between delivery time and customer satisfaction
- Performance under different weather conditions
- Differences between order channels
- Relationship between repeat customers and revenue
